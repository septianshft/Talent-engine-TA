2. Simple Additive Weighting (SAW) Method

SAW Overview

SAW is a Multi-Criteria Decision Making (MCDM) method that:

  • Assigns weights to different criteria
  • Normalizes scores to a common scale
  • Calculates weighted sums for final ranking
  • Provides mathematically rigorous results

Mathematical Foundation:

SAW Score Formula
Si = Σ (wj × rij)
Where: Si = Total score for alternative i
wj = Weight of criterion j
rij = Normalized rating of alternative i on criterion j

Normalization Process:

// Min-Max Normalization (for benefit criteria) r_ij = (x_ij - min(x_j)) / (max(x_j) - min(x_j)) // For cost criteria (lower is better) r_ij = (max(x_j) - x_ij) / (max(x_j) - min(x_j)) // Result: r_ij ∈ [0, 1]

3. Critical Competency Theory

Critical Competencies Concept

Critical competencies are skills that are absolutely essential for a role. Unlike regular competencies, they act as "gate-keepers" where insufficient performance results in automatic elimination regardless of other qualifications.

Key Principles:

🚨 Veto Power

Candidates below veto threshold are automatically eliminated

⚡ Enhanced Weight

Critical competencies receive bonus multipliers

🎯 Business Logic

Maximum 70% of competencies can be critical

📊 Configurable

Veto threshold: 60-100% of required level

Critical Competency Logic
Step 1 - Veto Check:
effective_veto_threshold = (required_level × veto_percentage) / 100
IF talent_level < effective_veto_threshold THEN ELIMINATE

Step 2 - Bonus Calculation:
IF is_critical AND talent_level >= required_level THEN
  score = score × CRITICAL_COMPETENCY_BONUS (1.2)
